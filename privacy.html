Puncto is local-first. We do not create user accounts, operate a Puncto backend, or use analytics or advertising trackers in the app.

1. Overview

Wartle (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Puncto, a punctuality streak tracker for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains how Puncto handles information when you use the app and this website.

By using Puncto, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information stored on your device

Puncto stores the information you choose to enter in the app locally on your device. This can include:

  • your name or profile details, if you add them;
  • saved destinations, including a place name, address, coordinates, and arrival radius;
  • routine settings, target arrival times, reminders, streak history, achievements, and app preferences; and
  • an optional profile photo you select from your photo library.

This information is used to provide Puncto’s features. It is not sent to a Puncto server because Puncto does not operate one.

3. Location, notifications, and photos

Location. If you grant permission, Puncto accesses your approximate or precise location only while the app is open. It uses that location to check whether you are within the saved arrival area at the time of a check-in. Puncto does not request background or “Always” location access, and it does not transmit your location to us.

Notifications. If you grant permission, Puncto schedules local reminders on your device. You can change this in your device settings or in the app.

Photos. If you choose a profile photo, Puncto requests access to select a photo. The selected photo is stored locally in the app’s storage and is not uploaded to us.

4. Puncto Pro purchases

Optional Puncto Pro subscriptions and purchase restoration are handled through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, as applicable, and RevenueCat, our subscription-management service. These providers process purchase and subscription information necessary to confirm your entitlement. We do not receive or store your full payment card information.

The Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat handle information under their own privacy policies. Your use of those services is subject to their terms and policies.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent, or share your Puncto routine, check-in history, profile photo, or location information for advertising or marketing. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in Puncto.

Purchase-related information is processed by the app-store platform and RevenueCat as described above. We may disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

6. Retention and deletion

Your Puncto information remains on your device until you reset it in the app, remove it, or uninstall the app. You can use Puncto’s in-app data reset controls to remove local app data. Removing the app generally removes data stored in its app container; platform backups may retain information according to your device and account settings.

7. Children’s privacy

Puncto is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address the concern.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Puncto changes or as legal requirements evolve. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email support@wartle.app.
